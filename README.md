# RNGwars

# [Play now!](https://zhubat.uqcloud.net/)

3-8 players

### How to play:
1. Players are given a random selection of words. Use these to compose a poem within 2 minutes.
2. You will then be shown the poems of your opponents. Vote on your favourite!
3. Scores are distributed based on how many players vote for your poem. Points are worth double in the second round and triple in the third round. It is always anyone's game!
